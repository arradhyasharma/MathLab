What is a polynomial?

A—01

A polynomial is an algebraic expression made up of variables and coefficients, where the powers of variables are non-negative whole numbers.

For example, the expression below is a polynomial in one variable.

Remember The highest power of the variable with a non-zero coefficient determines the degree of a polynomial.
