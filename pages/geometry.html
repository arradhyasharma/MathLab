THE FOUNDATION

Understanding geometry

G—00
01

Angles

Angles measure the amount of rotation between two rays. A triangle always has an interior angle sum of 180°.

02

Perimeter

Perimeter is the total distance around a closed two-dimensional shape.

03

Area

Area measures the surface enclosed by a two-dimensional geometric figure.
